## ✖Characters

**Associated List:** -
**Definition:** -

**Check (2026-10-26):** the class *comics character* (Q1114461) is no base: it marks anyone who ever appeared in a comic. Among its 177 members with 15 or more sitelinks outside DC and Marvel, the top are Mickey Mouse, Donald Duck, Darth Vader, Popeye, Yoda and Sonic. Our 10 are title characters of 10 franchises (Asterix and Obelix share one), and 9 of them carry P1441 to their own comic (*Peanuts*, *Asterix*, *Hellboy*, *Garfield*, *2000 AD*, …); Spawn has none. Lucky Luke's item has 5 sitelinks, Corto Maltese's 6, Invincible's 10: the series items hold the fame, not the characters.
**Structure (Trummler, 2026-10-27):** as for Disney, the topic gives way to franchise nodes, `comics/<franchise>/characters.json`, one for every franchise with a clean base (its comic via P1441 / P1080 / P8345, like an animated series). There is no size bar and no `others/` folder: the grouping into *Others* happens at render time. A visibility ruler (an eye as its handle) on the category node (*Comics*, *Anime*, *Animation*, *Film & TV*, *Gaming*), between the title and the word count, folds every child franchise that holds no entry down to the chosen tier into one *Others* node. It needs no new schema field: the children share their tiers already (`inheritsUpwards` requires identical `tierConditions` across the family), so a small franchise's top tiers are simply empty. Corto Maltese starts at the tier its fame earns, not at tier 0 next to Superman, and that tier decides where it folds. The build is #35's (or a later PR's).
**Size of the franchises** (Gemini, Deep Research, 2026-10-27; estimates, named / well-known), a guide to where each will fold: Asterix ~400 / 10, Peanuts ~70 / 10, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles ~250 / 10, The Walking Dead ~300 / 10, The Smurfs ~100 / 10, Archie Comics ~350 / 10, The Adventures of Tintin ~350 / 8, Spirou & Fantasio (with the Marsupilami) ~250 / 8, Invincible ~180 / 8, Popeye ~60 / 8, Scott Pilgrim ~40 / 8, Lucky Luke ~300 / 7, Hellboy ~200 / 7, Judge Dredd ~500 / 7, Mafalda ~15 / 7, Garfield ~30 / 6, Spawn ~200 / 6, Calvin and Hobbes ~12 / 6, Dilbert ~30 / 6, Gaston Lagaffe ~60 / 6, The Phantom ~250 / 6, Corto Maltese ~120 / 5, Thorgal ~150 / 5, Bone ~60 / 5, Blake and Mortimer, Valérian and Laureline, Tex Willer, Dylan Dog (4 well-known each), Diabolik (3). Measured at ≥ 15 sitelinks: TMNT 9, Tintin 8, Asterix 6, The Walking Dead 5, Peanuts 4.
**Official lists (checked 2026-10-27):** tintin.com has a static page per character, 100 in all (`tintin.com/en/characters/…`), and archiecomics.com 23 (`archiecomics.com/characters/…`); the pages of Asterix and Diabolik build their lists by script, while thorgal.com/personnages is static (10 characters, one page each), Peanuts' answers 404. popeye.com's About page names the main cast (Olive Oyl, Bluto, Wimpy, …). For the rest no official list was found; of the fan wikis Gemini offered as the next best (Deep Research, task 5), only those of The Walking Dead (`walkingdead.fandom.com/wiki/Comic_Series_Characters`), Scott Pilgrim, Hellboy and Spawn exist. A run on this task alone (`_data/Prompts/done/Comics_Character_Lists.md`) added three reference lists, names as text: Peanuts (`fivecentsplease.org/dpb/castlist.html`, a fan site's cast list), TMNT (`turtlepedia.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_characters`, about 240, all media together) and the Smurfs (`smurfs.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Smurf_characters`, about 240, with a chart of who is in the comics and in each animated series). The European franchises run alone (`_data/Prompts/done/Comics_Lists_European.md`) found a page for each: official ones for Lucky Luke (dargaud.com, a section on the main characters), Gaston (gastonlagaffe.com/univers.html), Thorgal, Mafalda (quino.com.ar, one sentence naming six) and Corto Maltese (an article on his women, not a list), and the French fan wiki's character category for Spirou (110 pages).
**Batch** (`2026-10-29_european-comics.txt`, 42 commands, imported and verified 2026-10-29, all 69 lines): from those pages, 10 new items (Thorgal: Gandalf the Mad, Slive, Nidhogg, Guardian of the Keys, their English names ~ from the English editions; Mafalda: Guille, Libertad; Gaston: Mademoiselle Jeanne, Yves Lebrac, his cat and his seagull) and the reference for the P1441 of 15 existing ones; Prunelle gets P1441 *Gaston*, and Jules-de-chez-Smith-en-face (Q2131946) his French name, where the Dutch one stood. Louve's two items are merged into Q2228081 (Trummler, 2026-10-29). The English names of the new Thorgal and Gaston characters were checked by a regular Gemini request (`_data/Prompts/done/English_Names_Check.md`): only *The Guardian of the Keys* (album 9's English title) is confirmed, nothing contradicts the others, so the labels stand.
**Batch** (`2026-10-29_lucky-luke-historical.txt`, 4 commands, imported and verified 2026-10-29): the series' own versions of the historical figures Dargaud's page names as met in it (Calamity Jane and Billy the Kid with a section each, Jesse James and Sarah Bernhardt in its introduction; a mention is enough, Trummler). Each is an item of its own with P1441 *Lucky Luke*, P1080 its universe and *fictional analog of* (P1074) the real person, as for South Park's portrayals: P1441 allows no human as its subject.
The American and British franchises run alone (`_data/Prompts/done/Comics_Lists_American_British.md`) found a page for each: official ones for Invincible (skybound.com, the two leads only), Judge Dredd (2000ad.com, four characters) and Calvin and Hobbes (Andrews McMeel, the two title characters), fan wikis for Garfield (garfield.com closed in 2020), Dilbert, The Phantom (phantomwiki.org, 379 pages) and Bone (83 pages). **Batch** (`2026-10-30_comics-official-us-uk.txt`, 16 commands, imported and verified 2026-10-30): the official pages as reference for P1441 of the 8 characters they name; Judge Anderson, Rico Dredd and Judge Giant had no P1441 to the strip *Judge Dredd* (Q60977215) yet.
**Tintin against tintin.com:** 34 of the 100 sit in the Tintin universe (Q25930860, P1080), 4 are doubled by a musical's item, and a few more exist under another name (Mohammed Ben Kalish Ezab, Laszlo Carreidas, Philippulus); about 55 have no item at all, mostly one-album characters. tintin.com titles most of them in French, but each page's text gives the English name (surname and first name apart), the occupation and an appearance by album, page and panel (for Chicklet: Trickler, R.W., *The Broken Ear* p. 35), so the page itself is the source for their creation in the node's own pass. The panel shown is one appearance, not necessarily the first (Boris's is from *Explorers on the Moon*, his first album is *King Ottokar's Sceptre*). Gemini's table of the 56 (Deep Research, `_data/Prompts/done/Animation_Anime_Comics.md`, Answer 2c) has 51 English names right; a checklist, the page is the source.
**Batch** (`2026-10-29_tintin-other.txt`, 52 commands, imported and verified 2026-10-29, all 366 lines): the 52 characters of tintin.com's "other" pages that have no item; Aristides Silk and Marco Rizotto have one already, under their English names. Each gets the English name from the page text as its label, the page title (French) as the French label, P31 *comics character* (the Bird brothers: *group of fictional characters*), P1441 the series (Q744536) and the album the page names, and P1080 the Tintin universe, all referenced to the character's page. Where a page names two forms, the other goes in as an alias (Colonel Jorgen / Colonel Boris, Chiquito / Rupac Inca Huaco).
**Rating:** ✖️ as one topic, since there is no class or link that holds "comics outside DC and Marvel"; per franchise the bases are clean.
