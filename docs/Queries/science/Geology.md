## ☑Minerals

**Base:** under *mineral species* (Q12089225), *mineral group* (Q1936581) or *mineral variety* (Q429795).
**Tiers:** sitelinks.

- **Varieties and groups belong in.** Species alone would miss ruby, emerald, sapphire, amethyst, feldspar and garnet, the names a reader knows best. As they rank with the species, they need no tier of their own, only `omittable` rules `mineral-varieties` and `mineral-groups` for those who want species alone.
- **Jade (74)** is in none of the three. Rocks like granite are rightly out.
- **Floor:** from 20 sitelinks, 359 entries; below that, names only mineralogists know.
- **Hardness** (P1088) is on 935 species, no tier measure but a possible info field.

**Rating:** clean. One gap to read through (jade).
